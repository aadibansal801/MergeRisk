# Week 3 — Separate test levels

Journal week: 29 Aug–4 Sep 2026

Source record period: 31 August–14 September 2026

I tried following the invoice through the diagrams and asking what I would check at each step. At the extractor, I'd look for a third required parameter. At the analyzer, I'd check what call it paired with that definition. At the command line, I'd check whether I could find the relevant line from the result.

That helped me explain the difference between unit, integration, and system tests with an example from the project. If the first check passes and the second doesn't, I can ask about call resolution. If both pass and the final report is hard to use, that gives Tanush and me a specific frontend follow-up. The tests have a job besides adding to the count.
