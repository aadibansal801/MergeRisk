# Week 3 — Define the reporting handoff

Journal week: 29 Aug–4 Sep 2026

Source record period: 31 August–14 September 2026

While we were drawing the September sequence, I kept noticing how much the report depended on what the analyzer gave it. If the page only gets a sentence, it has to guess where the branch or the location is buried in that sentence. That seemed like a good way to get inconsistent results between the terminal and the web UI later.

I wanted the view to receive the issue, the affected code, and its source locations as fields. The engine could settle what the finding meant. I could work out how to show it. That made the handoff clearer for me and Aadi and gave us a shared example to open when the browser stopped displaying something the engine had found.
