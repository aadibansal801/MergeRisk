# Week 5 — Walk the September review example

Journal week: 12–18 September 2026

Source record period: 14–17 September 2026

For the design review I had several diagrams I could show, and I was losing the point when I moved through them too quickly. I went back to the old invoice call. With the example in front of me, I could use a diagram to show where the source went or how the report got produced, without having to explain every arrow.

That also gave me a rough order for an eventual screen. Show what the person asked us to compare, show what happened, and give them a place to find the evidence. I wanted the person seeing MergeRisk for the first time to understand the problem before making sense of badges and risk labels.
