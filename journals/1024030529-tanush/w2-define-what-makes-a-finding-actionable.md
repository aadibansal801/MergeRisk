# Week 2 — Define what makes a finding actionable

Journal week: 22–28 August 2026

Source record period: 15–30 August 2026

A big severity label looked like the obvious thing to put in a report. When I tried to read it as a reviewer, the label only told me which row to look at next. It didn't tell me where to click or what to look for when I opened the file.

The changed signature was a good guide. I needed the function name, the location of the call, and the fact that `tax_rate` was now required. Keeping those pieces separate let the heading stay short. More importantly, it gave us a way to check the report without arguing about styling: could a person find the line and explain why that call was a problem?
