# Week 8 — Turn the prototype journey into screens

Journal week: 3–9 October 2026

Source record period: 4–14 October 2026

I came back to the terminal example and tried to picture where I would hesitate in a browser. With four files, the labels tell you which is which. With two branch names, you can easily put them in the wrong order. I wanted the role of each input visible while the reviewer was typing it in.

The result screen had a similar question. Give me the main outcome first, and let me get to the finding and its location without hunting. Those decisions survived changes to how the app receives repositories. On the hosted version, the form and merge diagram answer the direction question up front, and the report can concentrate on what the comparison returned.
